FAQ

Common questions about GEO

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring an organization's existing knowledge so that AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot cite it when users ask relevant questions. Unlike SEO, which optimizes for search engine rankings, GEO optimizes for AI citation and recommendation. Learn more about GEO.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO optimizes content for search engine rankings: position on a results page. GEO optimizes content for AI citation: being mentioned, quoted, or recommended when someone asks an AI assistant a question. People increasingly ask ChatGPT or Perplexity instead of searching Google. GEO ensures your expertise appears in those answers. See the full GEO vs SEO comparison.

Does Geode generate content with AI?

No. Geode surfaces existing expertise. The methodology extracts, restructures, and publishes what an organization already knows, in formats AI systems prefer to cite. We do not generate new claims, fabricate expertise, or create synthetic content. Every published surface traces back to verified organizational knowledge.

What AI platforms does GEO target?

Geode optimizes for citation across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, and Gemini. Each platform has different citation behaviors and preferences. The methodology accounts for platform-specific optimization while maintaining content integrity. Learn how AI citations work.

How long before results are visible?

AI citation behavior responds to structured content faster than traditional SEO. Initial citation changes can appear within weeks of publishing optimized surfaces. Sustained citation presence requires ongoing monitoring and iteration. We provide baseline measurements so improvement is quantifiable.

What industries does Geode work with?

Any organization with deep domain expertise that is underrepresented in AI-generated answers. Professional services, engineering firms, specialized manufacturers, consultancies, B2B companies. If your prospects are researching vendors through AI, GEO applies to you.
