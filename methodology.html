The Shift

Research behavior has changed

When a VP of operations needs a specialty contractor, they don't Google "best firms near me" anymore. They ask ChatGPT. When a procurement manager evaluates vendors, they ask Perplexity to compare options. The answer either mentions you or it doesn't.

This is not a future trend. It is current behavior. The companies that appear in AI-generated answers are capturing attention that used to flow through search results.

The question is not whether AI will influence how your prospects find you. It already does. The question is whether you show up.

The Methodology

Five steps. One principle.

The principle: perception before generation. Understand what exists before producing anything new. Every step extracts from your reality. Nothing is fabricated.

Citation Gap Audit

We ask AI platforms the same questions your prospects ask. "Who are the best firms for X?" "What company specializes in Y?" "Compare providers of Z." We document every response: where you appear, where you don't, what competitors are cited, what's inaccurate. This is your baseline. Everything else is measured against it.

Knowledge Extraction

Your organization has decades of expertise captured in proposals, whitepapers, case studies, technical reports, and SOPs. We process this corpus using deterministic classification, not AI generation, in the extraction step. The output is a structured map of what your organization actually knows, verified against source documents.

Content Architecture

AI systems prefer to cite content that is structured, authoritative, and directly answers questions. We reorganize your extracted knowledge into these formats: question-answer pages that match how people query AI, structured data markup that machines can parse, and reference pages that establish topical authority.

Surface Publication

Citation surfaces are deployed as web pages optimized per AI platform. Each surface includes schema.org structured data, semantic HTML, and content structured to match the citation patterns of each target platform. This is not SEO trickery. It is making your real expertise legible to AI systems.

Citation Monitoring

We track your citation presence across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, and Gemini. Monthly reports show citation count, position, accuracy, and competitor comparison. When a surface isn't performing, we iterate. When a new relevant query pattern emerges, we build for it.

Integrity

What we don't do

We don't generate content with AI

Every published surface traces back to your verified organizational knowledge. We extract and restructure. We don't fabricate. If your corpus doesn't contain expertise on a topic, we don't invent it.

We don't game AI systems

Prompt injection, citation spam, and synthetic link networks are short-term hacks with long-term consequences. We make your real expertise legible to AI systems. That's the sustainable approach.

We don't make claims without data

Every assertion about citation improvement is backed by measured baseline-to-current comparisons. If we can't prove it moved, we don't claim it moved.

Start with the baseline

You can't improve what you haven't measured. A Citation Gap Audit shows you exactly where you stand today.
